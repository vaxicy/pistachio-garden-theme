Pistachio Garden Theme brings a quiet garden calm to Chrome: a muted green frame, a soft pistachio toolbar, and a creamy New Tab page, with deep green text that stays easy to read.

Flat, solid colors only — no wallpaper, gradient, or texture. Active and inactive window frames share the same green, so the browser never shifts tone when you switch windows, and the New Tab wordmark uses Chrome's single-color theme mechanism to blend with your browser.

The theme declares no permissions and leaves the pages you visit untouched. Switch back to the default look at any time from Chrome's Appearance settings.
